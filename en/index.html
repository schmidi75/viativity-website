Why does motion sickness occur?

This unpleasant feeling arises from a sensory conflict: Your eyes report a standstill (e.g., when looking at your smartphone inside a vehicle), while your balance organ simultaneously senses the movement. The brain sounds the alarm.

How Viativity supports you

With smart immediate help and preventive training, the app helps your body actively resolve this contradiction. You learn to gently fade out disturbing stimuli and calm your nervous system again – completely without medication.
