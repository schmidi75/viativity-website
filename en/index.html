Sound familiar?

You look forward to your vacation, but motion sickness (kinetosis) or seasickness quickly spoil the ride. This unpleasant feeling arises from a sensory conflict: Your eyes report a standstill (e.g., when looking at your smartphone inside a vehicle), while your balance organ simultaneously senses the movement. The brain sounds the alarm.

This is exactly where Viativity comes in

With smart immediate help and preventive training, the app helps your body actively resolve this contradiction. You learn to gently fade out disturbing stimuli and calm your nervous system again – scientifically proven and completely without medication.
