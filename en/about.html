About Me

Viativity™, an independent app from Switzerland.

Hello, I'm Dieter, the mind behind Viativity.

Viativity is not an app from a giant tech corporation, and I am not a classical software developer either. I built this project entirely out of personal interest and fascination in my free time.

How it all started
The idea was born from an article in the Swiss newspaper 20 Minuten. It presented a study describing how a specific 100Hz tone can reduce motion sickness. This inspired me so much that I decided to build my own app with the help of artificial intelligence (AI).

However, I quickly realized: An app with just a single tool is not enough. So I dug deeper: What other scientific studies are out there that show methods to counter motion sickness (kinetosis) or discomfort while traveling? Based on these findings, I thought about how I could technically implement these approaches for a smartphone. In addition, I searched for established travel tips that have proven themselves in practice.

The result are the tools you find in Viativity today. As an individual, my focus is 100% on a fair product that accompanies you, hides no subscription traps, and absolutely respects your privacy.

On my social media channels (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube), I show you clearly how Viativity works in practice. You will find practical approaches for typical travel challenges and visual explanations on how to optimally use the tools on your journeys. You can find the links at the very bottom of this page.

Your feedback is my motivation

If Viativity has helped you to look out at the sea on the ferry relaxed again or to experience your flight more lightheartedly, I would be thrilled to receive a short review – you can do this easily right inside the app. Do you have suggestions, requests, or criticism? Feel free to write me a message anytime via the support link at the bottom.

Radical Transparency & Cooperation

A modern project like Viativity is created today through the combination of human creativity and innovative tools. It is important to me to communicate this openly and honestly:

  • Concept & Development: The ideas, scientific research, and overall concept come from me. For the technical implementation, programming, and copy design, I was supported by artificial intelligence.
  • Design & Graphics: The illustrations and visual elements were partially generated with AI support to create a consistent, calming experience for your journey.
  • Audio Content: While the technical frequencies (like the 100Hz modulation) are generated directly in the app, the atmospheric background sounds come from the great open-source community freesound.org.
  • Localization: To make Viativity accessible to as many people as possible, I used artificial intelligence for translations into other languages. Since I do not speak these languages myself, the direct benefit for you was my priority – rather than a flawless translation. Please forgive any linguistic hiccups!
