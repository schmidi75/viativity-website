What does the name Viativity mean?

Viativity is a play on words: It combines "Via" (Latin for way) with vitality and activity.

The name represents the core goal of the app: Not just enduring the trip to your destination, but being able to experience the journey itself as an active, positive part of your vacation again.

What is kinetosis and how does the queasy feeling arise on journeys?

Many people know this: Kinetosis is the technical term for the phenomenon mostly known in everyday life as motion sickness or travel sickness. It arises when the eyes, the muscles, and the balance organ in the inner ear send conflicting movement signals to the brain – for example, when reading a book in a moving car or being below deck on a rocking ship.

💡 Good to know About one in three people experiences motion sickness at least occasionally in their lifetime. Children between two and twelve years of age are particularly often affected.

The science behind it: This sensory conflict triggers a misinterpretation in the brain that disrupts the autonomic nervous system, leading to a strong queasy feeling and general travel discomfort.

Are the tools in the app scientifically proven?

Viativity is strictly a wellness app and not a medical device. It does not provide diagnoses and does not replace medical treatment. However: Nothing in the app is invented.

All integrated digital tools and methods are based on established findings in travel medicine and balance research. The curated travel tips combine proven home remedies with the official recommendations of international health organizations like the WHO and the CDC (Centers for Disease Control and Prevention).

Viativity simply translates this complex knowledge into easy-to-use companions for your wellbeing on the go.

Is the app a medical device or an alternative to medication?

No. The app is clearly positioned as a wellness app and is expressly not a medical device. It makes no medical promises.

It uses principles based on scientific approaches (such as visual references, breathing exercises, or stimulating the sense of balance) to gently support you while traveling. For severe kinetosis, the app does not replace medical advice or trusted travel medication, but serves as a smart, drug-free addition for your well-being on the go.
